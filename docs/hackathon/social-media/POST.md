## Post

I got tired of opening a tab for every funding story and then guessing which company might need a freelancer. So I built a desk that has to keep the source next to the claim.

Global Opportunity Intelligence stores public signals and asks seven agents to work them. Discovery, company research, technical reading, qualification, strategy, and quality assurance each have a job. I talk to the orchestrator. They share one Switch room. Mattermost is where I type. Telegram gets the finished report. Nobody gets an email unless I draft it, and a draft is still unsent.

The result I trust is a company the desk refused to pitch.

Stitch.ai launched an AI workspace. The first score was 76, and the suggested service was “LLM integration and RAG.” The source is a Hacker News launch post. I kept the 76. The second reading is business 70, technical evidence 25, freelance fit 0. Quality assurance rejected the service. The lead is still in review. Commercial intent is indirect: the post describes a product, not a purchase.

A later run discovered 14 headlines, researched 4, and qualified 0. Switch marked that run delivered. Telegram marked the report delivered. The older 14 high-priority labels are still on the dashboard. They came from the first scorer, and I have not rewritten them.
