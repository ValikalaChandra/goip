# Technical research methodology

Technical evidence is taken from stored source text. A new fetch of company sites, engineering blogs, and job pages was not run for the 22 leads.

A finding is an observed requirement only when the text states the requirement and the quotation occurs in that text. An AI assistant, a product launch, or a funding headline is not an LLM, RAG, API, or data-platform requirement. Those readings stay hypotheses or unknown.

The categories the research can use, when the text supports them, are AI and machine learning, data engineering, backend and APIs, and cloud and operations. They are not default recommendations. The current service matcher looks for those terms in text that was already classified as a requirement, a vacancy, or stated API or model language. An unsupported label does not increase freelance fit.

For this database, the technical-need field on each opportunity is an inference written by the earlier research pass. The preview found no observed requirement on the linked sources. Further research means a second source that states a task, a vacancy, or a contract. It does not mean inventing a stack.
