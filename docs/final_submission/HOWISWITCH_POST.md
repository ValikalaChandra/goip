# #HowISwitch

I got tired of treating a product launch as a freelance lead.

GOIP watches a few public sources, stores the sentence next to the company, and asks three separate questions: how notable is the event, is a technical need actually written down, and could an outsider honestly read this as paid work. Seven agents split that work. They do not all talk at once. An orchestrator posts one stage into a Switch room, the specialist does the work in the application, and the result comes back in the same room. I can type there. Switch is the room, not the model.

The example I keep is Stitch.ai. The old score is 76. The later reading is business 70, technical 25, freelance fit 0. Quality assurance rejected the service recommendation. The company stayed. Nothing was sent.

That is the product. A desk that can research a company and still refuse to pitch it.
